- **Music.** Your Plex music library is in the sidebar, with Artists, Albums, Songs and
  Playlists. Music opens on a wall of your newest albums and Plex's music rows; albums, artists and
  playlists have their own pages, and Songs is a sortable table. Search and Quick Open find
  artists, albums and songs (Return opens, ⌘Return plays).
- Gapless playback, with loudness leveling that keeps an album's own dynamics when it plays in
  order. Plays count on your server like any other Plex player.
- The Now Playing bar stays at the bottom of the window while you browse. The Controls menu and
  the media keys play and pause (⌥Space), skip songs (⌘← ⌘→), jump 10 seconds (⌥⌘← ⌥⌘→) and
  set the volume; every Music key can be changed in Settings ▸ Shortcuts.
- Starting a film or episode pauses the music. While music plays the Mac stays awake, but the
  display can still sleep.
- Small grey text (row numbers, column headings) is a little brighter, easier to read.
